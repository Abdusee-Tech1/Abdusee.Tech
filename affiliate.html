What Is an Affiliate Link?

An affiliate link is a special tracked URL that lets companies know a visitor came from our site. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase (or sign up for a service), we receive a small commission — typically between 5% and 30% of the sale price, depending on the program.

This commission comes from the merchant's marketing budget. You never pay more because of an affiliate link — in many cases, we secure discounts or free trials for our readers.

FTC Disclosure Compliance

In accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines under 16 CFR Part 255, and similar regulations in other jurisdictions, we are required to disclose when we have a material connection to products or services we recommend.

This disclosure page, combined with in-article disclosures where affiliate links appear, fulfils this requirement. We believe in complete transparency with our audience.

Our Editorial Standards

We take our editorial integrity very seriously. Here is our commitment to you:

  • Honest recommendations only: We only recommend tools and services we have personally used or thoroughly researched and believe are genuinely valuable to our audience.
  • No pay-to-play: We do not accept payment to include products in our tutorials or rankings. Affiliate relationships do not influence which tools we cover or how we review them.
