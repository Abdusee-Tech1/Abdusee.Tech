1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit, such as your preferences and actions.

Cookies serve many purposes — they make websites work more efficiently, provide analytical data to site owners, and can enhance the user experience by remembering your preferences between visits.

Cookies are not programs and cannot carry viruses or install malware. They are simple text files that can be read only by the server that placed them.

2. How We Use Cookies

Abdusee Tips uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask every visit
  • Store your theme preference (dark or light mode)
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve content and performance
  • Detect and prevent technical errors and security issues
  • Enable embedded content from trusted third parties (e.g., YouTube videos)

We do not use cookies to:

  • Serve targeted or behavioural advertising
  • Track you across third-party websites
  • Sell your data to data brokers or advertisers
  • Build personal profiles for marketing purposes

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the Site to function. They cannot be disabled. Without them, core features (such as cookie consent management) would not work.

Preference / Functional Cookies

These remember your choices to personalise your experience. For Abdusee Tips, this includes your dark/light theme preference, stored in LocalStorage (not a traditional cookie, but functionally similar). These expire when you clear your browser data.

Analytics Cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about how visitors use the Site — including which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, and how they navigate between sections. We have enabled IP anonymisation, so your full IP address is never stored.

Embedded Content Cookies

Pages on our Site may include embedded content from YouTube. When you interact with embedded videos, YouTube may set its own cookies. These are subject to Google's cookie and privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We have no direct control over cookies set by third-party services embedded on our Site. The following third parties may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website performance analytics — Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube: Video embeds — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (may log IP for CDN purposes) — Google Privacy Policy

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Local Storage & Session Storage

In addition to cookies, Abdusee Tips uses LocalStorage — a browser-based storage mechanism — to save your preferences (theme, consent) on your device. Unlike cookies, LocalStorage data is never transmitted to our servers. It exists only on your device and is cleared when you clear your browser storage.

7. How to Control Cookies

Via Our Cookie Banner

When you first visit Abdusee Tips, a cookie consent banner will appear. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your choice is saved and respected on future visits.

Via Your Browser Settings

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note that disabling all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites, including Abdusee Tips.

8. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable essential cookies, the following features will be affected:

  • Your cookie consent preference will not be saved and the banner will reappear each visit
  • Your dark/light theme preference will not persist between sessions

Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to read tutorials or use the Site. We will simply have less data to improve content and performance.
