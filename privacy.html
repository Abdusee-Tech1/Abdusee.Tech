1. Who We Are

Abdusee Tips ("we," "us," or "our") is a technology education platform founded and operated by Abdusalam Ahmed Kasim. Our website is located at abduseeTips.com.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Newsletter subscription: Your email address when you subscribe to our weekly tips.
  • Contact form: Your name, email address, subject, and message when you reach out to us.
  • Comments & community: Any content you submit in community discussions (if enabled).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Analytics: Aggregated usage statistics including page views, session duration, and click-through rates.
  • Device data: Screen size, device type, and browser capabilities for performance optimization.
  • Local Storage: Theme preferences (dark/light mode) and cookie consent stored on your device only.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Aggregated analytics from Google Analytics (anonymized, IP truncated).
  • YouTube embed interaction data when you watch embedded videos.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes, each with a lawful basis:

  • Sending newsletters (Legitimate Interest / Consent) — to deliver the weekly tech tips you signed up for.
  • Responding to enquiries (Contract) — to reply to your contact form messages.
  • Improving the website (Legitimate Interest) — to analyze usage patterns and fix technical issues.
  • Security (Legitimate Interest) — to detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive behavior.
  • Legal compliance (Legal Obligation) — to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use the following types of cookies. You can control non-essential cookies via our Cookie Banner.

  • Essential cookies: Required for site functionality (e.g., cookie consent preference). Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference cookies: Store your theme choice (dark/light mode). Stored in LocalStorage on your device.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. IP addresses are anonymized. Requires your consent.
  • Embedded content cookies: Set by YouTube when you interact with embedded videos. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

See our Cookie Policy for full details and opt-out instructions.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted processors who assist us in operating the website (e.g., hosting, email delivery), under data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Abdusee Tips, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data would be transferred with notice.

7. Data Retention

  • Newsletter emails: Retained until you unsubscribe. Deleted within 30 days of unsubscribe request.
  • Contact form messages: Retained for up to 2 years to handle follow-up communications.
  • Analytics data: Retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default), then automatically deleted.
  • LocalStorage data: Stored on your device; cleared when you clear browser data.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time via the link in each email.
  • CCPA (California): You have the right to know what data we collect and to opt out of data sale (we do not sell data).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at abdusalema0@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Abdusee Tips is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Security

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
